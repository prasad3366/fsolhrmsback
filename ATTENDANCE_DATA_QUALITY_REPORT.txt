ATTENDANCE DATA-QUALITY REPORT
==============================

Scope
-----
Read-only foundation analysis for Attendance, AttendanceRecord, and
AttendanceLog. No attendance data was inserted, updated, upserted, or deleted.

Canonical-source confirmation
-----------------------------
AttendanceRecord is the intended canonical operational source.

AttendanceRecord consumers:
  - AttendanceService history, today status, summaries, regularization,
    current punch paths, and administrative reads.
  - DashboardService.
  - ReportsService.
  - PayrollService and PayrollScheduler.
  - Employee 360 attendance through AttendanceService.

Legacy Attendance consumers:
  - AttendanceService.punchIn() fallback branch.
  - AttendanceService.punchOut() fallback branch.
  - AttendanceService.clockIn()/clockOut() do not use the legacy model.
  - AttendanceLog is written by the canonical punch path and the legacy
    fallback branch.
  - No other backend service read or write path was found.

Historical functionality remains represented by the legacy model and was not
deleted or migrated. Its rows remain readable and are reported independently.

Checks implemented by prisma/attendance-data-quality.ts
--------------------------------------------------------
  - duplicate employee/user plus business-date records;
  - null punch combinations and PRESENT without required punches;
  - zero-hour PRESENT rows;
  - clock-out before clock-in;
  - non-positive completed durations;
  - legacy/canonical date, status, and punch mismatches;
  - invalid AttendanceLog IN/OUT ordering;
  - duplicate IN and OUT logs;
  - inactive users/employees;
  - dates outside nullable employment start/end dates;
  - malformed canonical identity references;
  - open records carrying a final status.

Execution status
----------------
The HRMS Jest watch tree was holding the Prisma query-engine DLL. Only that
local process tree was stopped. The normal Prisma client was regenerated and
the analyzer completed successfully against the configured PostgreSQL database.

The analyzer is intentionally query-only. No attendance data was modified.
